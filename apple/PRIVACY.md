# GymFree Privacy Policy

Effective date: 5 October 2026

GymFree is a free iPhone and iPad app for planning and logging workouts, with optional calorie and food tracking. It has no ads, no in-app purchases, no accounts, no analytics and no servers. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. "We" means the independent developer who publishes GymFree on the App Store.

## The short version

- **We don't collect any data.** Nothing you enter in GymFree is sent to us or to anyone else.
- **Your data stays on your device.** Workouts, routines, body weight, food logs and health-check answers are saved only in GymFree's storage on your iPhone or iPad.
- **Apple Health is optional.** If you connect it, GymFree reads and writes only the data types you allow, and that data never leaves your device through GymFree.
- **Location is used only while you track a walk**, and the route is kept on your device.
- **No third parties.** GymFree contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting code, and makes no network requests.

## What GymFree stores, and where

Everything you enter is stored in GymFree's private storage on your device:

- your routines, plans and workout history (exercises, sets, weights, reps, notes);
- your body-weight entries;
- if you use the plan builder, your answers to its questions, including the health check (for example, whether you have a heart condition or are pregnant). These answers only change the plan GymFree suggests;
- if you turn on calories & food, your answers to its questions (such as age, height, weight, activity, goal and the health check) and the foods you log;
- your settings.

We cannot see any of this. There is no GymFree account and no GymFree server. Like any app's data, it is included in your device backups (iCloud Backup or a computer backup) if you have turned those on in iOS; Apple encrypts those backups and GymFree has no access to them.

## Apple Health (HealthKit)

Connecting GymFree to Apple Health is optional. If you connect it, iOS asks you which data types GymFree may read and write, such as workouts, body weight and walking routes, and you can change this at any time in the Health app or in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

- Data GymFree reads from Apple Health is used only to show your own information in the app (for example, your body weight).
- Data GymFree writes to Apple Health is your own logged activity, such as finished workouts.
- Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, never shared with third parties, and never stored by GymFree in iCloud.
- Disconnecting Apple Health stops GymFree from reading or writing it. Data already in Apple Health stays there until you delete it in the Health app.

## Location

If you track a walk, GymFree asks for permission to use your location, and uses it only while that walk is being tracked, to measure its distance and route. The route is saved on your device and, if you have connected Apple Health and allowed it, saved to Apple Health as part of the workout. Location is never used at any other time, never sent to us and never shared. You can turn location access off at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → GymFree.

## Notifications, Live Activities and alarms

Rest timers, Lock Screen Live Activities, widgets and the optional rest alarm are created on your device. They are not push notifications, and nothing is sent to a server.

## Files you export or import

When you back up or export your data, GymFree creates a file and hands it to the place you choose (for example Files, AirDrop or another app). When you import, GymFree reads only the file you pick. What happens to an exported file after that is up to you and the service you saved it to.

## Links to other websites and phone numbers

GymFree includes links (for example to support helplines, open-source licences and the source code) and tappable phone numbers. Opening one takes you to Safari or the Phone app; the websites and services you reach have their own privacy policies.

## Crash reports from Apple

GymFree contains no crash-reporting or analytics code. If you have chosen in iOS to share analytics with app developers (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may share anonymous crash reports and usage statistics with us. This is controlled by you and handled by Apple under [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/); we use it only to fix bugs.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your data is kept on your device until you delete it. You can:

- delete everything in GymFree with Settings → Data → Reset everything;
- turn off calories & food in Settings, which stops the feature;
- delete the app, which removes all of GymFree's data from the device;
- manage or delete Apple Health data in the Health app.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete, correct or hand over on request, and no consent to withdraw other than the permissions you control in iOS.

## Children

GymFree does not collect data from anyone, including children. Calorie and food targets are not offered to people under 18.

## Health information

GymFree gives general fitness and nutrition information. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition. See Settings → About → Health & safety in the app.

## Changes to this policy

If GymFree's data practices change, we will update this policy, change the effective date above and describe the change in the app's release notes. The history of this file is public in the source code repository.

## Contact
